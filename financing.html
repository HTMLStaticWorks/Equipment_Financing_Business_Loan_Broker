Commercial Financing Directory

Compare our six core financing vehicles. CapitalForge Finance analyzes your company’s unique operational requirements and identifies lending partners with suitable appetite, collateral flexibility, and competitive terms.

Asset Secured

Equipment Financing

Enables commercial enterprises to procure machinery, technology, diagnostic systems, or fleet vehicles while spreading payments over the asset’s productive economic life.

Typical Use Case: Purchasing manufacturing CNC machinery, heavy excavators, medical imaging units, or commercial refrigeration without paying 100% upfront cash.
Key Considerations: Down payment requirements typically range from 0% to 20% depending on equipment type and credit profile. Potential Section 179 tax deductions may apply.
Terms: 12 – 84 Months Full Details
Direct Ownership

Equipment Loans

Secured commercial installment loans where the buyer assumes direct title and ownership of the equipment immediately upon closing, with the equipment serving as principal loan collateral.

Typical Use Case: Acquisition of permanent commercial vehicles, specialized production plant machinery, or specialized farm equipment intended for long-term retention.
Key Considerations: Fixed monthly amortizing payments provide predictability. Lien is released upon final loan maturity payment for consideration.
Terms: 24 – 72 Months Full Details
Liquidity & Operations

Working Capital

Flexible liquidity funding designed to smooth day-to-day cash flow gaps, manage payroll surges, purchase bulk supplier inventory, or bridge seasonal revenue fluctuations.

Typical Use Case: Staffing up for major enterprise contract fulfillment, purchasing seasonal raw materials ahead of peak market demand, or managing receivables delays.
Key Considerations: Underwriting focuses heavily on consistent monthly bank deposits and operational cash flow rather than real estate or physical collateral.
Terms: 6 – 24 Months Full Details
Revolving Reserve

Business Line of Credit

A pre-approved revolving capital pool that allows enterprise owners to draw funds when needed, repay on flexible terms, and draw again without reapplying.

Typical Use Case: Serving as an ongoing financial safety buffer for emergency repairs, short-term vendor discounting opportunities, or unpredictable project expenses.
Key Considerations: Interest only accrues on the active drawn balance. Unused credit lines generally incur minimal or zero maintenance fees depending on lender terms.
Revolving 12 – 36 Months Full Details
Enterprise Scale

Commercial Financing

Larger capital facilities structured for institutional-grade projects, warehouse acquisitions, plant expansions, multi-million dollar equipment overhauls, or strategic refinancing.

Typical Use Case: Commercial real estate upgrades, major facility buildouts, acquiring complete manufacturing facilities, or corporate restructuring.
Key Considerations: Involves detailed debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) analysis and formal financial statement audits. Bespoke amortization available.
Facilities $250K – $5M+ Full Details
Expansion Focus

Growth Financing

Non-dilutive commercial debt customized to finance revenue acceleration milestones, market entry into new states, or strategic investments without forfeiting enterprise equity.

Typical Use Case: Opening a second or third location, investing in large-scale marketing campaigns, or building out sales distribution for typical networks.
Key Considerations: Structured to balance debt service obligations against projected new revenue ramp-up scheduled structure for commercial growth financing.
Terms: 12 – 60 Months Example Structure
