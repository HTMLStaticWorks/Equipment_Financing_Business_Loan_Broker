What Is Commercial Equipment Financing?

Equipment financing is a dedicated commercial debt or lease structure designed exclusively to fund the acquisition of tangible business equipment. Rather than paying 100% cash out of operating capital, the business enters into an agreement where the equipment serves as the primary collateral backing the funding.

Because the lender holds a security lien on an asset with resale value, equipment financing frequently offers more accessible credit criteria and lower interest margins compared to uncollateralized business loans.

Common Categories of Financeable Equipment

We arrange financing across a broad spectrum of commercial industries. Representative asset categories include:

Heavy Construction Excavators, bulldozers, cranes & loaders
Industrial & Tooling CNC machines, lathes & robotic arms
Healthcare & Diagnostics MRI scanners, surgical lasers & lab systems
Commercial Transportation Class 8 trucks, box vans, trailers & chassis
Commercial Culinary Walk-in freezers, hood vents & ranges
Enterprise Computing Rack servers, networking hubs & telecoms
*Not every asset category is universally eligible. Underwriting depends on market liquidity and asset condition.
