The CapitalForge Funding Process

A clear, 6-stage brokerage roadmap designed to minimize administrative friction, protect company credit standing, and navigate commercial underwriting with precision.

01

Initial Inquiry

Submit foundational information regarding your business, estimated financing requirements ($5K to $5M+), and commercial intent (equipment acquisition, working capital, or expansion).

02

Business Information

Provide verifiable supporting documentation: recent operating bank statements, equipment vendor quotes, or tax filings, uploaded through our secure encrypted portal.

03

Financing Review

A dedicated CapitalForge finance advisor evaluates your cash conversion metrics, debt capacity, and collateral profile against criteria across our network of lending partners.

04

Available Options

We present a side-by-side comparison of pre-qualified options from matching commercial lenders, clarifying monthly payments, total repayment costs, and covenants.

05

Review Terms

Work with your advisor to review final underwriting terms, clarify contract clauses, verify amortization tables, and confirm agreement details with no unexpected surprises.

06

Next Steps & Funding

Execute formal lender loan or lease agreements electronically. Capital is wired directly to your equipment supplier or deposited to your commercial checking account.

Important Compliance & Process Notice

Availability of financing, eligibility, interest rates, placement fees, covenants, and terms vary by participating lender, applicant creditworthiness, asset liquidation value, and state jurisdiction. CapitalForge Finance operates solely as an independent commercial finance broker connecting prospective commercial borrowers with independent commercial lenders. We do not provide guaranteed funding, guaranteed approval, or universal interest rate schedules.
