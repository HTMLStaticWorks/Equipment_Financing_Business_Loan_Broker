Structured Capital for Enterprise Endeavors

When a commercial initiative exceeds standard small-business lending limits, companies require institutional debt advisory. CapitalForge Finance connects mid-sized operators with private debt funds, non-bank institutional trusts, and specialty lenders capable of underwriting multi-million dollar facilities with bespoke amortization horizons.

We assist principals in presenting complex debt service coverage metrics, audited financials, and collateral packages to achieve favorable loan covenants and pricing structures.

Anatomy of a Commercial Debt Facility

Facility Parameter Typical Range Structural Role
Facility Magnitude $250,000 – $5,000,000+ Scalable based on enterprise EBITDA and verified tangible asset valuation.
Amortization Period 36 – 120 Months Extended terms allow significant capital projects to ramp up before heavy principal return.
Underwriting Focus Historical DSCR & Free Cash Flow Emphasizes multi-year tax filings, audited financial statements, and executive management.
Collateral Structure Hybrid Asset & Corporate Guarantee Frequently backed by specific enterprise machinery, accounts receivable, or corporate pledge.
