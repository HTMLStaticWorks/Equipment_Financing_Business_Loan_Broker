Revolving Capital Reserve

Commercial Business Line of Credit

Establish a revolving capital facility to draw on demand. Pay interest solely on what you borrow, repay at your pace, and replenish your available limit for ongoing enterprise security.

Inquire About Credit Lines

The Revolving Capital Access Concept

Unlike a closed-end installment loan where you receive a single lump sum and must pay interest on the full balance from day one, a commercial business line of credit works as an active, revolving facility. Once established with a maximum credit ceiling (e.g., $150,000), you can draw $25,000 today, repay it over three months, and have the full $150,000 available again for future opportunities.

Interest accrues strictly on the outstanding balance you have drawn. If your line sits idle at $0 drawn balance, no interest is billed, making it an ideal liquidity reserve against unforeseen market disruptions.

Common Strategic Uses
  • Supplier Cash Discounts: Seize 2%–5% early-pay supplier discounts.
  • Emergency Repairs: Rapidly fix broken machinery without waiting on loans.
  • Receivables Bridge: Fund operations during 60-day client billing delays.
  • Short Project Outlays: Mobilize job sites prior to first client milestone billing.
Draw & Repayment Mechanics
  • On-Demand Draws: Initiate draws into checking via portal wire or ACH.
  • Interest-Only Periods: Some lines offer interest-only monthly draws during initial use.
  • Replenishment: Principal payments instantly restore your available borrowing limit.
  • Renewal Cycles: Typical facilities renew annually subject to account review.
Costs and Terms That May Vary

Credit line pricing is dynamic and subject to independent lender criteria. Variables to consider include:

  • Draw Fees: Some lenders charge 1% to 2% on each draw transaction.
  • Annual Maintenance Fees: Facilities may carry nominal annual account fees.
  • Prime Rate Adjustments: Many commercial lines utilize a floating rate indexed to the Wall Street Journal Prime Rate plus a margin.

Enterprise Qualification Factors

Lenders evaluate specific criteria to determine line limits and rate margins:

Operating Track Record Minimum 12 to 24 months of verified active business history.
Consistent Gross Revenue Typical threshold of $25,000+ monthly deposits with healthy average daily balance.
Commercial Debt Ratio Existing active liens and debt obligations relative to net operating income.
Business Credit Profile Dun & Bradstreet, PayNet, and principal guarantor credit standing.
